# Policy SLA-CREDIT-01 — Service Credit Approvals

**Owner:** Finance (Nadia Osei)
**Applies to:** All customer contracts containing a service level commitment
**Last reviewed:** 2026-04-02

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure service credits are granted consistently, are financially controlled, and
cannot be used to make informal commercial commitments that the business has not
approved.

## 2. Baseline entitlement

Where a customer contract specifies a service credit, the **contract governs**. Where a
contract is silent, the following defaults apply:

| Incident severity | Qualifying condition | Default credit |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Unavailability 30–120 minutes | 10% of Monthly Recurring Charge |
| P1 | Unavailability greater than 120 minutes | 20% of Monthly Recurring Charge |
| P2 | Unavailability greater than 4 hours | 5% of Monthly Recurring Charge |

Credits are calculated per calendar month and never exceed the contract's aggregate cap.

## 3. Goodwill credits

A "goodwill credit" is any credit granted **above** the baseline entitlement, whether or
not the customer has requested it. Goodwill credits are commercial retention spend and
are subject to the approval matrix in §4.

## 4. Approval matrix

| Credit value (as % of Monthly Recurring Charge) | Required approvals |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Support Lead + Account Executive |
| 10% – 20% | Above, plus Finance |
| Above 20% | Above, plus **CFO** |

## 5. Verbal commitments

**§5.1** A credit discussed verbally, in a meeting or in chat, is **not a commitment**
and must not be represented to a customer as approved.

**§5.2** No credit may be confirmed to a customer in writing until the approvals in §4
have been obtained and recorded.

**§5.3** Where a verbal figure has been given in error, the account owner must correct
it with the customer at the earliest opportunity. Silence is not an acceptable remedy.

## 6. Recording

All approved credits must be recorded against the contract identifier and the
originating incident ticket, and reported in the monthly revenue review.

---

**Escalation:** Finance → CFO. Unresolved credit disputes are escalated to the CTO.
