## 1. Context

Bluepeak is our largest retail account. The renewal conversation has been brought
forward because of the 9 July outage. Procurement is now involved, which was not the
case in previous cycles.

## 2. July outage — Dana's position

Dana opened by saying the outage "could not have come at a worse time" — it landed in
the middle of the Back-to-School promotion, and her store managers lost visibility for
the better part of an hour. She was clear that she is not disputing our engineering
response; the acknowledgement was fast and the rollback was clean. Her concern is
**commercial**, not technical.

She has been asked by procurement to justify continuing with a single analytics vendor
after a P1 event. She needs something concrete to take back.

## 3. Commercial discussion

Marcus confirmed we are prepared to offer a goodwill credit against the July invoice.
Marcus stated on the call that **the Bluepeak renewal is worth $480,000 ARR** and that
we should protect it. He proposed a **25% goodwill credit** for July as a gesture of
good faith, to be confirmed in writing.

Dana said that would "go a long way" and asked for it in writing before her
procurement review on 1 September.

Priya flagged that a credit of that size would need internal approval and that she
would take it back to the team.
