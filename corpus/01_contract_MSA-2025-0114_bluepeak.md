# Master Services Agreement — Bluepeak Retail

**Agreement ID:** MSA-2025-0114
**Provider:** Kestrel Analytics, Inc.
**Customer:** Bluepeak Retail Holdings, LLC
**Signed:** 14 November 2025
**Effective:** 1 December 2025

---

## §1 — Scope of Services

Kestrel Analytics shall provide the **Kestrel Pulse** analytics platform, including
ingestion pipelines, the Pulse dashboard, and the Reporting API, to Customer and its
authorised users at 340 retail locations.

## §2 — Term and Renewal

**2.1** The initial term is **twelve (12) months**, commencing 1 December 2025 and
expiring **30 November 2026**.

**2.2** Renewal shall be automatic for successive twelve (12) month periods unless
either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to
the end of the then-current term.

## §3 — Fees

**3.1** Annual subscription fee: **USD 420,000** payable in equal monthly instalments
of USD 35,000 (the "Monthly Recurring Charge").

**3.2** Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes and are non-cancellable during a term
except as expressly provided in §4.

## §4 — Service Levels and Remedies

**4.1** Kestrel shall maintain **99.9% monthly uptime** for the Pulse dashboard and
ingestion pipelines, measured on a calendar-month basis.

**4.2** Where monthly uptime falls below the commitment in §4.1, Customer is entitled
to a service credit of **10% of the Monthly Recurring Charge** for that month. Where
the shortfall exceeds 120 cumulative minutes of unavailability in a calendar month,
the credit increases to **20% of the Monthly Recurring Charge**.

**4.3** Aggregate service credits in any contract year shall not exceed **30% of the
Monthly Recurring Charge** multiplied by twelve.

**4.4** Service credits are the **sole and exclusive remedy** for any failure to meet
the service levels in this section. Credits must be requested in writing within
thirty (30) days of the affected month.

## §5 — Support

**5.1** Kestrel shall provide 24×7 support for Priority 1 incidents and business-hours
support for Priority 2 and Priority 3 incidents.

**5.2** Priority 1 acknowledgement target: fifteen (15) minutes from report.

**5.3** Customer's designated technical contact is **Dana Whitfield, VP Operations**.

## §6 — Data Protection

**6.1** Kestrel shall maintain SOC 2 Type II certification for the duration of the term
and shall make the most recent report available to Customer on request.

**6.2** Any sub-processor changes require thirty (30) days' notice.
